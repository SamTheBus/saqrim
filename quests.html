What this quest collection covers

46 curated cards (including 24 new additions): named quests, encounter chains and explicitly labeled adventure-wide guides. This is not every quest in Skyrim or every quest in the recorded mods.

Completion rewards, choices, unlocks, boss loot, optional finds and unverified leads are different categories. A listed NPC outfit is not automatically a reward. A missing reward means not yet documented, not proof that the quest offers nothing.

Quest-start information combines the recorded PS5 menu, retained catalog research and newly consulted author or base-game references. Each card identifies its sources. PC version features and community routes are not independently verified PS5 behavior. Existing item effects and qualifications remain in the linked W-number entries.

Map buttons lead to existing geographic references or world guides, never invented coordinates. Nearby landmarks are clearly labeled. Reward names are visible; outcome details and walkthrough-like directions start collapsed.

All progress is manual and browser-local. Nothing reads your console, changes a save game, or marks a quest complete because an item is wanted.
