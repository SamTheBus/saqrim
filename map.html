Map sources, precision & unmapped items

The mainland layer remains unchanged. The Map / world selector opens the separate-world views; map availability and coordinate precision are identified for each realm. Explore other worlds.

364 mainland reference places come from a pinned exterior-cell atlas export plus its settlement reference points. Pins are approximate area markers, not surveyed entrance coordinates, interior floor plans or new PS5 gameplay verification. Selecting a place displays its approximate area.

Only reviewed associations connect catalog records to pins. Known-place markers with no linked items do not mean a location has no loot. Random drops, generic crafting, unspecified merchants and unresolved locations are never assigned made-up pickup coordinates.

Each item keeps its original research notes, version caveats and source links. Your browser-local Want / Maybe picks can be read here, but no console data or location tracking is used.
