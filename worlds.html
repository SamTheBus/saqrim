What the layers mean

Map pins are reference points, not exact pickup positions. A quest-start marker is not its final reward, and a vendor marker is not guaranteed stock. Individual directions remain attached to each item.

Icemoth’s map is stored on this site. The additional native-world terrain images are requested from UESP only when selected; a source host may block or fail to serve them. Solstheim’s coordinate pins remain usable without its background. Other worlds with unverified geography use explicitly labeled location guides instead of made-up maps.

Random loot and unverified placements are not turned into pins. The old Bloodmoon location names added by a mod are not assigned Morrowind-era coordinates on the Dragonborn map. Your choices are read from this browser; this page never changes them or reads the console.
