Before copying this setup · source and compatibility notes

This preserves Sam's current top-to-bottom order, reconstructed from the recorded 220-mod baseline plus Sam's 18 September rearrangement. It is not a proposed reorder or a guarantee that every mod is compatible. The Creation Club Content banner is not a separate numbered mod.

Recorded file sizes and menu versions are copied from the video and retained exactly. Where an official Bethesda listing has been matched, its current version and install size are shown separately; they may differ from the historical recording. The displayed 14.97 GB is the console's recorded total, not a sum of rounded rows.

Thumbnails are cropped from the actual Creations menu in Sam's supplied video, not newly retrieved Bethesda web listings. Generic placeholder images are preserved as shown. Names are the workbook's identifications; expand an entry for its clipped console title and recording timestamp. The same recorded thumbnails, sizes, versions, clipped titles and timestamps stay attached to their matching mods after renumbering. Bethesda-description panels use official Creations listings only; an unmatched entry is labeled as such instead of falling back to a Nexus description.

The mod now at #167 (The Great Cities of JK's North) has a recorded description warning against Lux / Lux Orbis, while Lux is now #214. The workbook also records an unresolved Thaumaturgy / Summermyst overlap (#25 / #26). These are retained cautions, not a crash diagnosis or instructions to change the order.
