About types, ratings and this catalog

The original 617-entry research archive is retained. This current view adds 16 records and explicitly revises the reviewed artifact summaries; original IDs and saved choices are preserved. They include spells, equipment families, services and uncertain leads—not 633 guaranteed unique artifacts.

Types and equipment slots are browsing labels derived from existing names/descriptions and cited author documentation. Unestablished schools or armor classes say Unknown. A full set is not automatically treated as an individually indexed helmet.

Base weapon damage is not enchantment damage. Base armor is not a bonus to armor, a whole-set total or a ward. Missing numbers are never replaced with zero. Author base values and your manually entered in-game values are sorted separately; unknown values go last in both directions.

Source values can differ from a PS5 port or from your character's displayed values. Personal values are entered by you, not read from the console. Choices use the existing browser storage key and old choice backups remain supported.
